# Playbooks Glossary


## Playbook

A manual that provides detailed instructions for operational actions during security events.


## Incident Response

The process of identifying, containing, and recovering from security incidents.


## Vulnerability Response

The process of identifying and addressing security weaknesses.


## Business Continuity Plan (BCP)

A plan that allows an organization to continue operating after a disruption.


## Living Document

A document that is continuously updated based on new information and changes.


## Preparation

The phase where organizations establish procedures and resources before an incident occurs.


## Detection and Analysis

The phase where security teams identify and investigate potential incidents.


## Containment

The process of limiting the impact and preventing further damage from an incident.


## Eradication

The process of removing threats and malicious artifacts from systems.


## Recovery

The process of restoring affected systems back to normal operation.


## Post-Incident Activity

Activities performed after an incident to learn from the event and improve security.


## Coordination

Sharing information and reporting incidents according to organizational and legal requirements.


## SIEM

Security Information and Event Management.

A platform that collects and analyzes security logs to detect threats.


## SOAR

Security Orchestration, Automation, and Response.

A technology that automates security response actions.
