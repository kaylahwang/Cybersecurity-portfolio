# ISO/IEC 27037

## Overview

ISO/IEC 27037 is an international standard that provides guidance for the identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation of digital evidence.

The goal of the standard is to ensure that digital evidence remains reliable, authentic, and admissible throughout an investigation. It establishes a consistent methodology for handling evidence while preserving its integrity.

---

## Purpose

The standard assists organisations and investigators in:

- Identifying potential digital evidence
- Collecting evidence from digital devices
- Acquiring forensic copies of data
- Preserving evidence integrity
- Supporting internal, civil, and criminal investigations

ISO 27037 is relevant to incident responders, forensic investigators, security professionals, and organisations that manage digital evidence.

---

## Core Processes

### 1. Identification

The process of searching for, recognising, and documenting potential digital evidence.

Examples:

- Hard drives
- Mobile devices
- Memory cards
- Network logs
- CCTV systems
- Cloud storage

---

### 2. Collection

The process of gathering physical devices and media that may contain digital evidence.

Examples:

- Seizing a laptop
- Securing a USB drive
- Collecting a smartphone

---

### 3. Acquisition

The process of creating a copy of digital evidence while preserving the original data.

Preferred method:

- Bit-by-bit forensic imaging

---

### 4. Preservation

Maintaining evidence integrity and protecting the original state of collected evidence.

Methods include:

- Secure storage
- Restricted access
- Hash verification
- Documentation

## Important Concepts

### Digital Evidence

Information or data stored or transmitted in digital form that may be relied upon as evidence.

Examples:

- Emails
- Browser history
- Log files
- Registry entries
- Memory dumps
- Network traffic

---

### Imaging

Imaging creates a bitwise copy of digital media.

Unlike normal file copying, forensic imaging captures:

- Active files
- Deleted files
- Unallocated space
- File system metadata

---

### Hash Values

Hash values are used to verify evidence integrity.

Common algorithms:

- MD5
- SHA-1
- SHA-256

If the hash value changes, the evidence may have been altered.

---
